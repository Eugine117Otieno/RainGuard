What RainGuard Does

🧺

Catches litter

An angled mesh guides some floating rubbish into a removable basket.

📏

Shows water levels

A simple float indicator helps demonstrate when water is rising.

♻️

Supports cleaning

The removable basket makes collected rubbish easier to inspect and remove.
