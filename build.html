How to Build RainGuard

Use simple, low-cost materials to create a small demonstration model.

Materials Needed

  • Plastic tray or cut plastic bottle
  • Plastic mesh
  • Small plastic container for collecting litter
  • Wooden sticks and cable ties
  • Small sealed bottle or cork for the float
  • String and coloured card for the indicator
  • Clean water for testing

Building Steps

1

Prepare the channel

Use a plastic tray to represent a small drainage channel.

2

Install the mesh

Attach the mesh at an angle so it guides floating litter towards the collection basket.

3

Add the collection basket

Place a removable container where the mesh guides the rubbish.

4

Make the float indicator

Place a sealed small bottle or cork in a separate side chamber to demonstrate water-level changes.

5

Provide a bypass

Include an open route for water to pass if the screen becomes covered.

6

Test your model

Use clean water, paper scraps and leaves. Observe the flow and improve your design.

Ask an adult to help with cutting tools. Never enter a real drain or test the model with sewage or contaminated water.
