About RainGuard

RainGuard is a school environmental project that demonstrates one way of catching floating rubbish before it travels further through a drainage channel.

The Problem

Plastic bags, bottles and other rubbish can collect in drainage channels. This can reduce water flow and contribute to flooding, especially during heavy rainfall.

Our Solution

RainGuard uses an angled mesh screen to guide floating litter into a removable collection basket. A float in a separate chamber demonstrates rising water levels.

Benefits

  • Demonstrates how floating litter can be collected.
  • Helps learners understand water-level monitoring.
  • Encourages proper waste disposal.
  • Promotes environmental awareness.
Safety: This is a school-scale demonstration, not a ready-to-install public drain device. A blocked screen can worsen flooding. Real installations require engineering review, a properly sized bypass and regular maintenance.
